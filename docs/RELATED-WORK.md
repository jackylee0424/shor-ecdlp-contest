## Cost conversion: read this before the rankings

The prior lab often writes `T` for **Toffoli gates**, priced by a **sampled mean** over executed classical branches. Here `Tmodel = 7·#CCX + #P`; the adaptive cost takes a **maximum** at measurements. CCZ becomes `H; CCX; H` (7 T), CZ becomes `H; CX; H` (0 T), SWAP is three CX (0 T), and a quantum-controlled SWAP can use one CCX plus two CX (7 T). A measurement-conditioned Z should use `H; X; H`, rather than a paid `P`. Qubits are the union of distinct physical labels over the whole adaptive program; resetting a fresh label does not remove it from this union. [H/ShorECDLP/Framework/CostModel.lean; H/ShorECDLP/Framework/Quantum/Adaptive.lean, `tCount`, `wires`, `qubitCount`; H/ShorECDLP/Framework/Quantum/MeasurementUncompute.lean, `pauliZ`, `controlledZ`; P/research/R008-scaler-primitive/README.md.]

**Do not multiply a prior mean by seven and declare a resource bound.** S's C77 final emission reports 2,425,952 static CCX+CCZ versus expected 2,244,421.5 and sampled 2,244,389.598. Seven times the static count is **16,981,664 T per point-add packet**; seven times the sampled mean is only 15,710,727.186. The former is a conservative all-enabled gate-count proxy for the old stream, not a proved bound on its future `Straight` lowering: compound conditions, new controls and lookup may add gates. [S/scratch/r25/C77-emit-286710000000071.log, `POSTPASS_CTX final`; S/lp-variants/c77-final/src/point_add/mod.rs, `counts`; S/package/note-867r.md.]

For scale only, using 56 trials and 28 additions per trial gives 1,568 point additions. C77's static proxy would contribute **26,630,282,560 T**, including 54,168 Fourier phase slots per trial but **excluding lookup, extra controls, repairs, resets and proof-driven hardening**. At a guessed 883 wires (867 + a 16-wire address bank), the product is about **2.35e13**. With 464 binary-controlled additions per trial, the analogous arithmetic is **4.41e11 T** and **3.90e14 Q×T**. These are **conditional guesses**, not proposed valid entrants: the existing public-offset point add is not already a controlled, coherent table-selected point add. [Arithmetic: lab `workspace/related_work_costs.py`; repetition/window/phase counts: B/INTEGRATION.md, B/Window/StreamGateCounts.lean; prior interface: S/lp-variants/c77-final/src/point_add/frogtail_batch/pointadd_lilypad.rs.]

## 2. Port exact measurement erasure and phase-only computation

**Concrete source.** `G/source/arithmetic.py` has `carry_base_recipe`, `and_uncompute`, `compare_phase_exact` and `native_*phase` routines. `S/lp-variants/c77-final/src/point_add/ctx_holds.rs` tracks affine value forms and equal products, finds an idle provably-zero donor, replaces repeated CCX targets by CX from the cached product, then HMR-clears the donor and repairs with a transcript-controlled CZ. `S/.../mod.rs` applies `truth_simplify` before `ctx_holds`; the order and exact postpass output matter. Its emission reduces static nonlinear gates from 2,436,454 to 2,425,952. [S/scratch/r25/C77-emit-286710000000071.log.]

**Start with one exact identity.** If t starts at zero, compute `t=a∧b` using one CCX. Later X-measure/reset t; conditional CZ(a,b) cancels the sign for either result, provided the same a,b values remain available. Erasing this AND costs zero T instead of a second 7-T CCX, plus one measurement. Sharing k identical product uses costs one compute instead of k, saving 7(k−1) T; this needs a clean donor and proof that the product value stays equal. These savings remain valid on a worst branch because the correction is Clifford. [G/source/arithmetic.py, `and_uncompute`; S/.../ctx_holds.rs; R/opus-inv/gatecut/GatecutB.lean, `hmr_pay_from_function`.]

**Lean destination.** Prefer the bench's existing full quantum result in `H/ShorECDLP/Framework/Quantum/MeasurementUncompute.lean` (`measureResetWithCorrection_coherent`, `recomputeZCorrection_phase`) over re-proving Hilbert-space measurement. Prior `GatecutB.hmr_pay[_at][_from_function]` is a useful small contract: the intervening computation preserves f, a recomputation restores f, and the recorded correction cancels its sign. Its model deliberately omits the common 1/√2 amplitude; it is not independently a Born-mass theorem. The richer `F/lean/SpookyLever/Quantum.lean` supplies `mbu`, `clears`, `cond_f`, `window` and `deferred_fix` on ring-valued amplitude functions, with explicit no-read/transport conditions. It also drops the common measurement factor and compares equal-measurement branches. This is useful source-level quantum algebra, still requiring a bridge to the bench's normalized instrument. [R/opus-inv/gatecut/GatecutB.lean, comment above `clearLane`; F/lean/SpookyLever/{Quantum,Shot}.lean; H framework file.]

**Porting sketch.** Define a fixed `Straight n s (s+1)` AND-erase block: `.measure t` followed by a `recordGate` CZ decomposition. Prove run correspondence, restored scratch, T=0 for erase, one record and wire-support preservation. Compose with a small arithmetic block in Entrant. Only then consider a certificate-producing value-numbering pass: each proposed replacement should provide explicit equality/no-write/clean-donor premises and a local replay theorem. Prior Rust checks and random 64-lane validation are useful discovery tools, not trusted proofs. [H/ShorECDLP/Challenge/StraightLine.lean; B/Challenge/RegisterCorrection.lean; S/.../ctx_holds.rs.]

**Constraint on generalization.** The challenge allows one constant-bit or one earlier-record control per gate. The prior runtime supports stacks of compound classical conditions. A conjunction controlling CCX/CX/measurement needs a proved lowering (possibly a scratch wire storing record values), not an arbitrary callback. Static topology and fixed measurement cursors must survive. [H/ShorECDLP/Challenge/{StraightLine,Template}.lean; G/generation/runtime.rs.in, `guards`; R/review/P3/P3/Harness.lean.]

## 3. Port exact adders before approximate modular arithmetic

**Small, useful entry point.** `G/source/arithmetic.py:carry_base_recipe` computes each carry with one CCX and uses X-measure + CZ when unwinding it. `chunk_add` exposes widths and ownership; without a carry-out the unsplit n-bit ripple uses n−1 nonlinear carries, hence 7(n−1) T. The isolated 256-bit construction touches 512 operand wires plus 255 carries; a room-limited chunk planner trades carries for recomputation and boundary debts. This estimate excludes a quantum add-control, modular comparison/reduction and any output carry. [G/source/arithmetic.py, those functions.]

**The mature low-room example.** `R/review/P3/P3/Ops/N287.lean` fixes N=287, RF=18, RI=6, W=593, M=16. `P3/Prod/N287.lean` ties the actual constant-ONE production list to a folded controlled list and transfers forward/round-trip statements. The review found **852 CCX on the production pair versus 1,426 on the initially exported real-control pair**. Seven times these is 5,964 versus 9,982 T; the comparison illustrates why counting operations alone missed the wrong export. It does not establish that a forward/inverse pair remains valid with arbitrary arithmetic inserted between them. [S/scratch/r21/LEAN-REVIEW.md; R/review/P3/P3/{Fold,Prod/N287}.lean.]

**Porting sketch.** Reuse the bench's exact bitvector/integer specification and quantum MBU lemmas; prove the carry cell parametrically, then induct over the ripple/chunks. Track a debt predicate in the forward postcondition and prove the inverse from that predicate plus preservation of its inputs. Specialize constant controls with a `run_fold`-style theorem, retain wire disjointness/frame hypotheses, and prove count recurrences alongside execution. Use `B/Challenge/{GidneyAdd,RegisterCorrection,Sequence,Relabel,CursorShift}.lean` as examples of handling records without callbacks.

**What cannot be copied as a certificate.** `R/review/P3/P3/Thm/N287.lean` ends its large obligations with `bv_decide`; its native axioms are documented in `LEAN-REVIEW.md`. P1 and C53 likewise have successful local checks whose positive gate theorems rely on native LRAT evaluation. `leanchecker` also reported deep recursion for the large P3 artifact. Keep the exporter, symbolic executor and mutants for research; replace native leaves by structural proofs or admissible kernel proofs, and keep proof terms small enough to replay. [S/scratch/r21/{C48-report,C53-report,LEAN-REVIEW}.md; R/review/P3/P3/{Sym,Thm/N287}.lean.]

## Remaining constructions: what transfers and what does not

### Point addition, doubling, inversion and exceptional inputs

C77's wrapper explicitly uses two Bernstein–Yang walks and an MBU shell: form dx/dy, divide to obtain the slope, compute the new x using a square, multiply to form y, and recompute discarded values for phase repair. The final 867-wire plan's C74 component model attributes 619,333 expected Toffolis to forward walks, 980,483 to inverse walks and 644,605.5 to the shell; the repriced proxies are 4.34e6, 6.86e6 and 4.51e6 T. These phase totals cover the packet's aggregated phases, not one arbitrary inversion call. [S/.../frogtail_batch/pointadd_lilypad.rs; S/scratch/r25/C74-component-preview.json.]

Neither that wrapper nor the Gold point-add receipt is an inspected Lean proof of **total controlled group addition**, including identity, inverse, zero denominator and doubling. Do not insert a public-offset point-add result where the challenge requires a controlled table-selected translation. No separately certified secp256k1 doubling circuit was found in the surveyed proof inventory. Doubling could be built from exact field cells using slope 3x²/(2y), but its Q/T is **unknown** until its exceptional-input and cleanup design is priced. Shor window constants can be pre-doubled through coefficient scalars without a quantum doubling subroutine. [S/.../frogtail_batch/pointadd_lilypad.rs; G/source/arithmetic.py; B/REFERENCE.md, quarantined Figure-14 domain; B/Challenge/SharedTable.lean.]

`modp_ft.rs`'s product/inverse, signed add/halve/double, `mont_halve_k`/`mont_double_k`, `fold4` and `add_table` are real reusable algorithms. However its comments explicitly drop window carries, and the C77 note admits rate-based windows without pointwise containment, with correlated production-input inequalities open. The REDC-k source exploits p=2²⁵⁶−(2³²+977), deriving its quotient from finalized low output bits. Port that algebra, not its unsupported uniform-rate claim. [S/.../frogtail_batch/modp_ft.rs, `mont_win`, `mont_halve_k`, `redc_plan`; S/package/note-867r.md.]

### Lookup tables, windowing and low-qubit layouts

The prior `add_table`/mask/fold tables select **small classical arithmetic functions** and exploit XOR forms/ANF monomials. They are not the same as a quantum-indexed `(a_i G+b_i Q)` table. In this challenge the judge already supplies x/y/infinity/−x/−y/3x fields; prior `times3` and constant-loading work can sometimes be eliminated by reading those fields with `whenBit`, while coherent table selection still needs a proved network. [S/.../frogtail_batch/modp_ft.rs, `add_table`; G/source/arithmetic.py, `times3`, `coordinate`, `mask_unsplit`; H/ShorECDLP/Challenge/PointFields.lean; B/Challenge/{PointTable,SharedTable}.lean.]

Distinguish three kinds of “window”: (1) Shor signed scalar windows/coefficient tables, already present in B; (2) chunk-adder planning, which may be exact with repair; (3) truncated carry/compare/width windows, which introduce errors unless a support theorem closes them. The prior scalar-input circuit tests and nonces establish none of the full Shor sampling guarantees. Low-Q ring rotations, known-zero lane loans and payload reuse are worth porting only with frame/disjointness/support proofs. [B/Challenge/{SignedWindow,SharedTable}.lean; S/scratch/r21/LEAN-REVIEW.md; S/package/note-867r.md; G/NOTES.md.]

### Spooky checkpoint and tape compression: retain the dead ends

R005's measured-profile MSC saves 71 data wires in the multiply but leaves the divide at the whole-circuit cap; it adds about 65.2k Toffolis. Its divide junction lower bound is conditional on the SIR operations/widths, not a universal impossibility proof. The scaled TLA+ search is exhaustive for its **two-register finite model**, whereas the three-register case timed out. It cannot certify secp256k1 arithmetic or the optimality of all pebbling schemes. [P/research/R005-spooky-walk-on-e9cacd1/README.md; P/docs/spooky-theory.md; P/formal/tla/RESULTS.md.]

Later Gold E9 studies found a free-W1 tape language with 32·29^(k−1) ordinary k-tick words: ideal compression only about 21 bits over a 748-bit tape, with available rank/unrank circuits far too costly. This does not rule out another walk/representation or a better coder; it deprioritizes copying the existing codec into this challenge. Earlier general inversion “floors” also belong to their dated primitives/room/profile and must not be treated as global bounds. [G/notes/e9/e9-o21.md; G/notes/e9/e9-c25.md; P/research/R012-inversion-algorithms/README.md; P/docs/lever-search-guidelines.md.]

## Relationship to arXiv:2607.13816

**Conclusion from source structure: sub870 is a different arithmetic line, not an inspected implementation or demonstrated extension of the paper.** C77 names Bernstein–Yang divsteps, a 532-tick Lilypad schedule, half cofactor/value rings and digit erasure; its credits trace Lilypad/Lilypad-G, Frogtail and later contributor optimizations. The paper implementation uses a location/length-controlled Euclidean register-sharing construction and a fixed 1,620-microstep schedule. They share affine MBU/recomputation ideas, which is not evidence of direct lineage. No direct 2607.13816 implementation claim was found in the surveyed sub870 notes/final wrapper and R012 cards. This is a scoped negative finding and an architectural inference, not a search of every cached note. [S/.../frogtail_batch/pointadd_lilypad.rs; S/NOTES.md; S/package/note-867r.md; B/REFERENCE.md.]

The bench pins **v2 (9 August 2026)** and supplemental generator commit **e64aa3c1198d96aeb389e64bc7ae48edbb9712ec**, including file/table hashes and a corrected remainder lane for the executable schedule. Current arXiv is **v3 (5 September 2026)**, whose abstract advertises the 835-qubit construction; don't silently substitute it for the bench's pinned reference. The 854/8.0e11 numbers are the bench's legacy formalized streaming/repetition bounds, not simply the external abstract's headline. [B/REFERENCE.md; B/Window/{StreamGateCounts,StreamTightSupport}.lean; external primary sources: [arXiv version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13816), [pinned supplemental tree](https://github.com/ZeroWang030221/Quantum-Algorithm-for-Elliptic-Curve-Discrete-Logarithms-with-Space-Efficient-Point-Addition/tree/e64aa3c1198d96aeb389e64bc7ae48edbb9712ec).]

**Carries over to finishing B inside Entrant:** exact Clifford repair/AND erasure; local constant-control folding; paired carry-debt invariants; physical-label/owner accounting; source-to-program and program-to-resource ties; mutant and witness tests. **Does not carry over automatically:** Lilypad support bounds to the paper EEA, paper success bounds to a changed walk, the old reset/decoder positions after changing measurements, old means as T-model bounds, or the 835/854-wire number after adding exceptional corrections. The paper reference itself quarantines non-exceptional generator tests and stale cross-mode inverse cache counts; these are useful regression cases.
