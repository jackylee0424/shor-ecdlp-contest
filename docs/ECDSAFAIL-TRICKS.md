## How the width is packed, and what actually transfers

**Trailmix (~1150):** the apply pair alone is 512 wires; the walk trades shrinking u/v registers against a growing compressed transcript, with carry scratch consuming remaining cap. A source-owner census in `6c0e30c` finds 603 tape +256 apply y +256 divisor scratch +~37 auxiliaries at one peak. This is not a route to 660 merely by lowering the allocator cap. Its floor arguments are conditional on its own state and transcript, not global bounds on inversion. (`N/6c0e30c.md` §4; `N/92214c2.md` §2; `C/trailmix_ludicrous/{gcd,codec}.rs`.)

**Frogdrop (660–676):** discard/recover a member of the Euclid quartet, share payload and reciprocal workspace, and repeatedly park small quotient chunks in lanes that are zero only in the active frame. `f0bd623`'s accounting is **257 ring +128 Q +259 payload +8 sy +8 sx +1 ph +5 pool =666**; its narrower successor reports **257 ring +128 Q +258 payload +7 E +5 S +4 scalar +1 PH =660**. The logical payload can remain 259 bits through a proved zero and temporary owner. These are layouts with reuse, not four 256-bit registers somehow stored simultaneously in 660 arbitrary qubits. Their reciprocal/replay bill is the reason for 389M–662M means. (`N/f0bd623.md` width/lineage; `N/9a8bbca.md` direct layout; `L/b682/src/point_add/frogdrop.rs` invariant; `L/bk666/src/point_add/pointadd_frogdrop.rs`.)

**Lilypad/Greedyfrog (~807–867):** C77's two 291-bit rings include shared cofactor/value roles alongside a 256-bit passenger, small counter/log/digit banks and two flags. Later Lilypad cuts the log to four lanes and removes counter/ring owners, with exact recoding mixed with restricted support. Greedyfrog borrows payload and offset-length high lanes, changes short-lived scratch scheduling, and uses the idle width as a shell carry pool. `da2b459`'s top-bit assumption has 71 exceptions in 60M model walks; it is explicitly not an all-input invariant. (`S/package/note-867r.md`; `N/e35d2d1.md`; `N/da2b459.md` host/top-bit checks.)

**The port's 3,932 are largely a different problem:** 855 old/address labels (including overlooked wire 837), **5×513=2,565 endpoint words**, and **511 comparator scratch +1 flag=512**. Four source endpoints must remain available while matching is composed; a fifth word loads/unloads the current target. This is actual simultaneous storage in the chosen correction schedule, not 3,077 obviously dead labels. Windows/rounds already reuse these labels. Resetting fresh labels does not lower the global union. The 854→~870 arithmetic packing idea does not erase this bank. (`U/Challenge/CompleteSupport.lean`, `B/Challenge/WindowBody.lean::batchedResetWires`, `B/Challenge/BatchedEndpoint.lean::{batchWord,batchFlag,batchScratch,batchSchedule}`, `B/Challenge/BatchPlacement.lean`.)

The distinct transfer is to **track live values and borrow idle or dirty physical owners**, including proof of restoration. Brief 05's calculator identifies 310 old labels outside data/address/control that could host correction storage. That gives 3622 labels with unchanged T, conditional on placement/clean-entry/frame proofs. This report does not claim ~870 from a permutation of the existing batched circuit. More dramatic cuts require a different endpoint representation or a recomputation schedule. (`workspace/streaming_t_breakdown.py`; `docs/CONTRIBUTIONS-PLAN.md`; `B/Challenge/{BatchPlacement,BatchExecution}.lean`.)

### A concrete trade: delete the 511 clean comparison scratch labels

For a 513-bit register equality, the existing X/CX conjugations reduce equality to a 513-control X on its flag. Its clean V-chain costs **2·513−3=1023 CCX**. The exact dirty chain in `L/bk666/src/point_add/mask.rs::mcx_dirty` costs **4·(513−2)=2044 CCX** and restores all 511 dirty donors. When comparing target with endpoint a, take donors from endpoint b; when comparing with b, take them from a. The two endpoint registers are physically disjoint from target and flag. They can contain arbitrary data, even entangled data, because the chain is a reversible identity on every basis assignment and restores them. No measurements are added.

`endpointTranspositionCircuit` uses four equalities (two before and two after the update). Thus ΔT is **4·(2044−1023)·7=28,588 per transposition**, **457,408 per correction** (16 transpositions), and **12,807,424 per 28-window round**. Retain the one flag, remove all 511 clean scratch labels. Q becomes 3421; T/round becomes **14,406,182,065**, only **0.08898%** above the port. The corresponding 26-round values and the combined 3111-label prediction are in the opening paragraph. This exchanges a little time for width using exactly the mechanism the clean-bank entries trade in the opposite direction. (`B/Challenge/{EndpointTransposition,BatchedEndpoint,BatchCost}.lean`; `N/0ab84d9.md`, `N/55edbc4.md`; calculator `dirty_mcx_check` exhausts k=3..6 including all dirty donor states.)

## Alternative: binary semiclassical streaming with public-point additions

### Circuit and success contract

Use one reused phase-control wire. For each of 256 G bits and 208 Q bits, prepare |+>, apply the appropriate controlled translation by a known table point, apply the semiclassical Fourier feedback and measure/reset the phase wire. Coefficient rows are `(2^i mod order,0)` and `(0,2^j mod order)`: **464 rows**, independent of Q. Points depending on Q remain classical protected table values, not entrant callbacks. Binary unsigned powers can implement the same scalar function as signed windows; matching offsets and bit order requires a new execution/record proof. (`B/Challenge/{Coefficients,Outer,PointTable}.lean`; `B/Window/{StreamScalar,StreamFourierCommute,ReducedContract}.lean`; `H/ShorECDLP/Challenge/Template.lean::{PointCoeff,constantPoint,TemplateGate}`.)

Budget **464 controlled adds/round**, **54,168 feedback P slots/round** (=256·255/2+208·207/2), **26 rounds /12,064 adds**. This deliberately does not subtract the first addition or optimize initialization. With an **exact** realization of the same Fourier distribution and wrapped decoder, reuse the proved p ≥ 163/1000 and obtain **1−(837/1000)^26 = 0.990208214125169**. Six rounds under p=.546 is a separate unproved analytic improvement from Brief 05, not an architecture benefit. Redo record offsets even if the decoder parameters remain 256/208. (`U/Challenge/DecoderBridge.lean::wrappedIdeal_mass_lower`; `B/Challenge/OuterResources.lean`; `workspace/round_mass.py`; calculator.)

### Control costs without pretending measurements are controlled gates

An exact total translation A_K can be given one coherent control with an extra encoded point D initialized to infinity O:

1. Controlled-swap A and D when c=1.
2. Apply **one unconditional** A_K to D.
3. Controlled-swap A and D again when c=1.
4. When c=0, D contains K; clear its known encoding by `whenBit`-selected Clifford operations. When c=1, D contains O; restore the common clean O/zero encoding.

The result is `(A+cK, clean dummy)`. Two 513-bit controlled swaps cost **1026 CCX =7182 T**, plus **513 dummy wires and 1 control wire**, with no added measurements. Conditional constant-XOR clears are Clifford; infinity's canonical bit must also be handled. The construction needs A_K to be coherent with **input-independent branch coefficients** after repair, so internal measurements reveal neither A nor c. A basis-only classical correctness theorem does not suffice. The calculator verifies the functional wrapper on toy cyclic groups; quantum lifting remains a Lean task. (`H/ShorECDLP/Framework/Quantum/MeasurementUncompute.lean` coherence contract; `B/Arithmetic/PointCorrection.lean::fig14PointEncoding`; Fredkin decomposition inspected in `C/trailmix_ludicrous/mod.rs::cswap`.)

A direct integrated control could avoid the dummy and approach ~900–1300 Q, but its width and T premium are **unknown**. Blanket control costs can exceed 3–4 CCX per nonlinear gate and add CCX for CX/SWAP; controlling measured arithmetic additionally changes phase repairs. PAIR-CTRL may reduce that premium, but a seven-times-mean or arbitrary factor-of-two is not a worst-case bound. (`N/f0bd623.md` §4; `N/8cbf348.md` dirty echo costs.)

### Exceptional cases are required, including the control-off path

An affine two-division shell must be a permutation even at zero denominators, and then implement actual group addition at O, K, −K and −2K. The bench replaces zero denominators by 1, proves generic correctness outside this four-point set, and composes a finite matching correction. This includes doubling and infinity. Generic random-input tests rarely exercise those points; the dummy wrapper deliberately exercises **O on its entire c=0 branch**. (`B/Arithmetic/{PointCorrection,PointExceptions}.lean`, `fig14EncodedEquiv_nonexceptional`, `fig14CorrectionSwaps_correct`.)

For a hardened kernel realizing **the same** zero-replacement coordinate permutation, existing batched correction can be specialized to a singleton tree: nodes=0, so **573,216 T/add** (=16×35,826), no query-tree measurements, and no 32,768-row traversal. The protected menu includes the exceptional images and doubled-point fields, beyond the six fields listed in older lab summaries. They may be read by `whenBit`; no Q-dependent classical endpoint callback is needed. A different affine completion needs its own endpoint proof and may not be covered by that menu. (`B/Challenge/{BatchedEndpoint,BatchCost,EndpointEncoding}.lean`; `H/ShorECDLP/Challenge/Template.lean::PointField`; `PointFields.lean`.)
