`constantPoint_aliased`, `sources_coherent`, `actual_coherent`, and

lookup and window semantics. The specialized window proves:

- correction T 1,576,701 (was 3,067,645);
- complete pre-cancellation window T 511,823,095 (was 513,314,039);
- correction measurements 212,979 (was 425,971);
- correction slots ≤220,684,182 and complete window slots ≤2,000,000,000

There are nine, not seventeen, 15-bit windows in this leader: the left packing
is one direct 16-bit load, seven 16-bit signed windows, and nine 15-bit signed
windows; all fourteen right windows are 16 bits. Therefore the exact official
cut is 9×1,490,944 = 13,418,496 T, or 0.08733% of score. The existing
1,156,680-T cancellation applies to all thirty signed windows; new proof
anchors `Optimization.narrowCorePatch` through `narrowActualPatch` establish
that it also applies to the specialized body.

The 208-bit result explains why a radius-16 Fejér estimate is insufficient:
the concentration interval is centered on the exact fine phase, but the
accepted 32-outcome interval is centered on the rounded coarse phase and can
place the exact phase at its boundary.

A finite-order upper bound makes the 208-bit no-go rigorous without trusting
the limiting integral. Four residue strips next to the positive bin boundary
force an excluded outcome to have sinc² mass bounded below by `4/5`, `2/5`,
`4/45`, and `9/490`. Residue permutation and floor loss give forced average
failure `≥11449/1128960 - 1153/(882*order)`, hence right mass
`<0.989858808107` and combined mass `<0.984934137420`. The much tighter
0.966899899947 figure remains a diagnostic used to rank 209–212 bits.

The 211-bit option would need three new 17-bit lookup trees and a seventeenth
address wire (priced as 856 qubits); no finite-kernel averaged coarse-bin Lean
bound was completed. At 212 right bits, trimming the left axis 263→262 changes
one left 16-bit window to 15 bits and frees exactly the missing 16,384 rows,
meeting the 983,040 cap exactly. Its two-average union diagnostic is
0.994518580517, but it depends on both the unproved 262-bit left average and a
new 212→203 coarse-bin average. Patch 21 is therefore parked and its `apply.sh`
exits 3 without editing the bench.
