## Further levers

- **Per-round success:** `DecoderBridge.wrappedIdeal_mass_lower` uses protected `Window/Repetition.secpSuccessBound_numeric` to round the stronger symbolic bound `((order-1)/order) * (4/π²)^2` down to 163/1000. `Window/ReducedSuccess.reducedPhysicalVerifiedMass_lower` proves that symbolic bound for the actual verified distribution. It is approximately 0.164255716, so 163/1000 is conservative. However, certifying 25 rounds requires about 0.168236229; even the stronger existing symbolic bound is insufficient. Fewer rounds need a stronger analytical mass bound, a broader accepted event, or a changed phase-estimation design.
- **Space:** the full 3,931-wire reset list forces nearly all of the 3,932-wire support bound even if some arithmetic live sets are smaller. A narrower reset/support argument needs renewed cleanliness, retry independence, and record-index proofs; merely changing the declared bound is insufficient.
- **T:** per-window bounds retain a cheaper first lookup; 28 addition windows dominate. Improvements to arithmetic or tighter window costs must propagate through `actualWindowTCost`, `WindowBudget`, and the unconditional `UniformT` argument (resources must hold for every public point, including zero).
- **Slots:** retained the conservative 5,000,000,000,000 program-slot bound; reducing to 26 rounds leaves ample room under the official 4×10^15 cap.
