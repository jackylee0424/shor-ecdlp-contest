## What changed

The source is jackylee0424's 870 (b2e963af). Every edit is exact. Deltas are expected Toffoli of the emitted stream
from env-less builds at 870 (b2e963af itself: 2,238,045.0).

1. **Shell modular adds: the carry out is formed on the clean overflow flag** (-435.5). The chunked 256-lane add
   (`add_flag_u`) built its final carry on a pool lane, copied it to the overflow flag k and uncomputed it. k is
   clean on entry, so the final carry is now formed directly on k and kept. The last chunk can hold one lane more
   (at most P - nch + 2 lanes instead of P - nch + 1), so the measured-erase chunks below it are one lane shorter.
   Only the topmost chunk boundary moves (one lane down); the lowest boundary keeps its position.
2. **Exact nine-atom truth-table pass with product index**, run after the existing passes (-108.5).
3. **Exact conditional-AND pass**, options m, f, s, run last (-346.0): CCX whose AND equals one control's value
   become CX, ANDs read only by phases become Clifford phases, and ANDs read only under one classical bit are built
   under that bit.

**All together:** 2,237,609.5 expected Toffoli as built, 2,237,155.0 after the passes (-890.0). The trusted mean at the
shipped nonce is 2,237,177.215.

**In the source, inactive at 870** (from our 999 point, 17f1e23c; the stream is byte-identical with and without them,
checked by ops.bin SHA-256): the inverse eraser's out-of-place normalizer and held decrement borrows (on from a
89-lane walk pool; the 870 inverse walk owns one pool lane), its flag block held on carry-pool lanes (from 16 pool
lanes), and the saved paired boundary at lane 66 or above for two-chunk shell products (the 870 shell pools give
three or more chunks). The three owned normalizer lanes of our 999 point (X04) are off: they would put the inverse
walk at 871. The x-step square stays the closed flat half-square (P1 flat); the Karatsuba square with retained
scratch needs a 940-lane cap.
