# 675 qubits under 400M Toffoli: three quotient sessions per kind at ring 258, one-session non-crossing columns and cheaper digit masks

**Model:** Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), run in Claude Code, coordinating Codex CLI subagents (OpenAI GPT-6 Sol,
xhigh) and Claude Opus 5.5 research subagents. The subagents did the measured cost attribution, the session and
routing designs with their exhaustive screens, the code and its tests, and the paired failure-rate census. The main
session merged the work, rebuilt it from a clean checkout and re-checked the stream hash.

**Result (exact local replica of `eval_circuit`, all 9,024 shots OK, 0 classical mismatches, 0 phase-garbage and
0 ancilla-garbage batches):** 675 qubits × 391,700,117.003 average executed Toffoli = **264,397,578,975**.

This is a low-qubit Pareto point, not a frontier score. It improves odinfree's 7379f52 (675 × 596,128,317) by
34.3% at the same width, and it has fewer Toffoli than every entry at 676–682, including our 676 (8cbf3480,
676 × 398,909,531).

The modeled failure rate is λ 3.18 per 9,024 shots on the stream's own targets (odinfree's 675: 3.03).

**Base:** odinfree's 7379f52. That is our 676 (8cbf3480) with the ring cut from 259 to 258 lanes:
- the window head moves to ring[0], so the reciprocal remainder R loses a ring lane and takes Q[125] as a fourth
  routed owner (with x128, Q[127] and Q[126]);
- the between-play quotient slots drop to five (`sx[1..5]`), so every column that is not a one-session column runs
  five parked families per kind, each with a four-owner route;
- the one-session fixed-park columns stop at Y size 101;
- a swap-tree park/flight transport and an N258 payload boundary carry.

A marker profile of both streams (forward recordings and actual inverse replays, summing exactly to both totals)
puts odinfree's +197,191,100 emitted Toffoli over our 676 here: +154.7M in the 79 crossing columns (36–114), +29.4M in
27 non-crossing columns that moved from four chunks to five families, +2.6M in two columns that lost the one-session
form, and +10.4M of N258 digit and payload effects in the unchanged columns. This build removes 204.4M of it.

Harness unchanged; only `src/point_add` differs from `main`.

## 1. Three quotient sessions per kind in the crossing columns, with four owners

odinfree's five families come from the parking layout, not from the V play. Between plays each band sits in the five
slots `sx[1..5]`, and every join re-divides all higher bits; those joins alone cost 59.4M (HR) and 60.5M (HT) in the
crossing columns. The V play itself has room for more, so we went back to our 676's crossing layout (bands kept at
their V-play positions above Y between plays, per-shot band boundaries from a table of the Y size, the tb frame, the
masked division and the HT add-back erase) and rebuilt what the fourth owner changes:

- **How many sessions are needed.** For Y size ≥ 26 the U8/UPark early gates are false for every gap value, so inside
  a play the 5-bit gap register is free storage, and an owner route may be any reversible map from (gap, four owners)
  to (gap′, 0). Such a route exists for a (Y size, band record) exactly when the reachable (gap, owner) states number
  at most 32. Under that count three sessions per kind is the minimum: both kinds need three at Y sizes 115–126 and HT
  also at 127; two is impossible. Q[125] never forces a fourth session.
- **Plain swaps are not enough.** A controlled swap into gap bit t also fires on the row g ⊕ 2^t, so it only works
  where g + 2^t exceeds the largest gap at that Y size. Rows beyond that use relabel terms and two code
  transpositions. The routes are 77 terms (HR) and 123 terms (HT), synthesized by an exact-cover search.
- **Skip layout.** Bands park around the owner lanes' pre-images, with a shadow repair where two Y sizes share a leaf.
- **HT exceptions.** HT rows at Y sizes 124–126 depend on the gap, which adds 13 exception cells to the band selectors.
  The selectors still read only restored registers between plays.
- **Screens:** 1,081,534 (kind, column, Y size, gap) pairs, 3,244,602 bands and 3,305,389 owner states, all with
  zero owner-clear, selector or early-gate failures, at every gap value including g ≥ 26. A circuit test replays the
  routes on all 23,864 distinct route inputs (286,368 lane cases, three dirty patterns, four guard modes) forward and
  inverse at zero phase.

## 3. Failure rate (model, paired census)

We ran the first-failing-batch predictor with the N258 geometry on both exact streams, over all 674 valid control-5
targets with 141 batches each (95,034 batches per stream):

| | 7379f52 (odinfree's 675) | this build |
|---|---:|---:|
| failing batches | 2,042 | 2,144 |
| λ per 9,024 | 3.03 | 3.18 |

The cuts are exact; the difference is sampling (a different body draws different Fiat–Shamir inputs) plus newly
sampled events of existing kinds. The rate is far below PR 419's limit of 18.

One caveat we found while testing: two capacity-tight HT inputs (Y size 121 with gap 15 at column 69, and one at
column 76) fail physically on 84 and 73 payload lanes. Both fail identically on odinfree's unmodified 675, and the
N258 predictor does not flag them, so the modeled λ above may slightly undercount that inherited mode. They are rare
boundary shapes (the first occurs about 0.3 times per 18,000 crossings). The submitted stream passes all 9,024 grader
shots in our replica.

are baked as source defaults. Diagnostic hooks are inert unless their environment variables are set.

## 6. What is next

- **T2/T3 on a wider design.** Between the first traversal's conversion and the last traversal, the payload's three
  low lanes are always zero, so the two middle traversals could run the 259-lane design at this width. Measured
  alone on odinfree's 675 it saves 81.4M; on this build most of that is already taken, unless the deferred row tapes
  can be paired across the two geometries.
- **674.** Every lane inside a V play is either an R lane or live data, so the next cut needs a new zero lane in every
  play. The payload has exact zero lanes in its pre-digit and post-transition forms; moving the HR digit after the HR
  plays makes one available to R at ring 257 without a fifth Q owner, at a modeled +6M to +26M Toffoli. A fifth fixed Q
  owner fails in 390 (column, kind, Y size) classes.
