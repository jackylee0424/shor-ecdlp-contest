What Chartechnörzli changes

The inverse walk evaluates several small Boolean charts: functions of a handful of low cofactor, value and parity lanes that stand in for lanes the layout no longer holds. Until now each chart was emitted from its algebraic normal form, one multi-controlled toggle per monomial on borrowed dirty lanes. Three of them have a much shorter factored form.

The lane-2 role exchange toggles U2 by sigma times D2, where D2 = A xor U1 B with A = F2 xor P and B = G1 xor P, and P is the stable rho parity. Folding P into one parity lane, building A on the F2 lane and B on that parity lane by CNOTs, and applying two Toffolis around one controlled toggle costs 3 Toffoli instead of 11 on every inverse tick.

The eraser's lane-2 mask term regroups as e and not F2, xor e times the upper-zero test of the shift times a two-way select between not F2 and not D. One CSWAP on the shift's lowest bit puts the selected operand on the F2 lane, so the term costs 15 Toffoli instead of 33.

The carry into bit 3 of the zero-slack shifted cofactor add was a 35-monomial chart: 185 Toffoli to compute and a 77-term phase oracle to erase. It becomes a three-step carry chain once three intermediate values sit on lanes: e xor rho on the parity lane, X1 = F1 xor e xor U0 (e xor rho) on F1, Y1 = U1 xor U0 xor U0 (e xor rho) on U1, and their product on the clean carry lane the add uses next. The compute now costs 10 Toffoli and the erase 9, and the add's repaid carry phases at positions 1 to 3 become Z and CZ gates on those lanes. Every rewrite restores all lanes it touches and was checked against the original chart on every input of its variables.

What Ungradgänger changes

Grenzgänger lends the sign-copy lane that the boundary fix leaves behind to the even inverse tick's counter increment and cap update, which run before the role exchange. On odd inverse ticks the counter update ran after the exchange, where that copy no longer exists. Ungradgänger moves the odd tick's counter complement, increment and cap ahead of the exchange, exactly as the even ticks do. The exchange reads the counter only through the rho parity and only under sigma. The update changes that parity by sigma xor 1, so sigma times the parity is the same before and after. The freed copy lane becomes one more clean lane for the increment and the cap and is restored before the exchange. On this stack the odd tick also fuses its marker clear into the increment, and the fused form re-derives the increment's gate trace. It is given the same lane view so the trace still matches. Its one cofactor literal is the sigma host lane, which the exchange leaves unchanged; the code checks this at build time and keeps the original order otherwise. The change applies to the regenerated odd ticks 13 to S - 1, and the forward walk is unchanged.
