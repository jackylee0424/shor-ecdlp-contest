# Lilypad-GCD 860 at ring width 288: gnuchev's 867 with the 856 tree's ring cut plus our no-sigma cut — 860 x 2,308,062

**Result (local: BitWonka's fasteval, an exact replica of `eval_circuit`, on the grader's own 9,024 shots — all OK, 0
classical / 0 phase / 0 ancilla; the official evaluation is the grader's):** 860 qubits x 2,308,062.403 average executed
Toffoli. A low-qubit Pareto point. Before this row the lowest Toffoli at <= 860 qubits was 3,095,636 (our 860, `27835909`);
this row is 787,574 below it (-25.44 %). Against gnuchev's 867 (`37cded22`, 867 x 2,243,275), the tree this
row is built on: -7 qubits for +64,788 T (+2.89 %). It replaces our 860 (`27835909`, 3,095,636, built on the same tree with seven exact cuts) at the same width.

## What changed

1. **Ring width 291 -> 288 (an envelope change, disclosed: it raises the failure rate, see Lambda).** The 867 tree's
   Lilypad-G walk keeps two rings of W lanes (X = the x register + 35 lanes, Y = 291 lanes). We apply gnuchev's own
   schedule transform from his 856 row (`42dd64fa`, `sched_g`): W -= 3, every tick's cofactor window clamped to
   kc <= W - c, and from tick 450 one value lane moved to the cofactor (c -= 1, kv <= c, kc += 1 within W - c).
   The 867 tree's own schedule table, its tick count S = 532, its cap (q = 11, 13-lane Q word) and its 8-lane cap log
   are unchanged. `RLEN_S` (the endpoint cofactor C' lanes) follows the transform, 287 -> 285, the value gnuchev's
   856 row uses. Six qubits come off every walk phase (2 x 3 ring lanes).
2. **Product-shell room.** With the rings narrower, `PEAK0` is set to the new width; the shells' carry pool is the room
   left under it (`Ms::alloc_room`, floor 52 - (867 - `PEAK0`) as in our 866..860 rows), so it is smaller than at 867.
3. **Exact inverse-walk cuts from our earlier rows, measured from the cut geometry.** Our 866..860 rows built one
   patch stack with a width dial; here the dial reads `PEAK0` + 2 x 3 = 866, so the following cuts are active:
   - **No sigma lane in the inverse walk, and the L5 eraser's only temporary borrowed (our 866 row's cuts 1-2).** sigma is formed in af(t,0), which is |0> after both signed adds and is U0 of the next frame, where the K9 lane-0 swap leaves sigma anyway; the lane-0 swap becomes CX(U0, V0) and the sigma decode disappears. The eraser's temporary comes from a cleared sign lane, at the terminal tick from the endpoint copy af(t+1,2) of G = +-1 (the success support), and at zero-slack even ticks from the hosted layout (V0 as the temporary, normalizer bit 2 hosted in h3, which is |0> whenever e = 1). At this ring width there are more zero-slack ticks than at 291, so the hosted layout runs more often.
   Exactness check at this width: on 10 fixed-input corpora (90,240 identical inputs) this row and our ring-cut 861
   (same width, no cut) fail exactly the same shots (74 = 74, symmetric difference 0).
4. **Nonce constant.** `LILYPAD_TAIL_NONCE` 86,700,000,000,064 -> 4,111 (see Nonce).

## Lambda (measured; this row trades error for width)

The ring cut makes the walk fail on more inputs. Every failing shot we observed is a classical mismatch of the walk
envelope (mostly the end-of-walk cofactor outgrowing its 285-lane region), none phase- or ancilla-only.
- **Classical walk model** (LILY-B's port of the tree's Pm/Gm walk, set to this tree's q = 11, 13-lane Q word, 8-lane
  log): 3.01 +- 0.12 per 9,024 shots at the parent's W 291 (the parent measures 2.875 on 48 fixed-input corpora) and
  7.89 +- 0.19 at W 288 (4M divisions each; WidthCreg 5.48 of it). On this row's stream the model predicts every
  failing shot exactly: on the grader's shots at the k = 0 body 12 actual = 12 predicted, on one salted corpus 12 = 12 (no false positive or negative), and the failing sets equal our ring-cut 861's on 10 fixed corpora (that build: 106 = 106 on 12 more corpora).
- **Measured, unpaired:** 8 salted corpora (FE_SALT, 72,192 shots): 58 failing (7.25 per 9,024);
  the parent 30 on the same salts (3.75).
- **Measured, paired:** 10 fixed-input corpora (90,240 identical inputs given to both circuits): parent
  29, this row 74 failing; both 29, this row only 45, parent only
  0 (+4.50 per 9,024).
- So the true failure rate rises from about 3 to about 8 per 9,024 shots. The no-sigma cut adds no failure of its own (failing sets identical to the uncut ring width on the 10 corpora above). Our 860 row (`27835909`) bought its width with seven exact cuts at the parent's rate; this row buys most of it with the ring cut at about 2.5 times the rate and about 788,000 fewer Toffoli.

## Nonce

`LILYPAD_TAIL_NONCE` (the 49 identity X X pairs on qubits 0/1 after the passes) is 4,111: the first value in a
scan from 0 that the walk model predicts clean on the grader's shots (values 0..4,110 each have a predicted failing shot; 4,184 values tried in 24 s). The model prefilter computes
each candidate's Fiat-Shamir shots exactly and runs the classical walk on both divisions; the pick was then confirmed
by fasteval on the tail-patched stream and again on the rebuilt stream (0 failing). Before it, k = 1..1,174 extra X X
pairs (our 866..860 rows' nonce form) were scanned on the k = 0 body with no clean count; that form is not used here.
The nonce chooses which shots the grader draws; it does not change the failure rate above. Scans are logged in our
nonce ledger.

## What is measured, not proved

- The failure rate above is sampled (model on 4M divisions, replica on the corpora listed), not derived.
- The model's exactness on this stream is checked on the corpora listed, not proved; the clean pick is checked on the
  exact replica, which is what the grader runs.
- `RLEN_S` 285 makes the endpoint reduction's high part (`reduce_ct`) two lanes shorter, as in gnuchev's 856 row; its
  partial-reduction precondition is inherited from the 856 tree and not proved at a successful endpoint.
- The inherited unit tests run the parent's schedule; the evidence for this row is the measurement above.
- We did not attribute the T increase against the parent by phase (candidates: narrower shell carry pools and more
  zero-slack ticks, where the eraser runs in its hosted forms).
