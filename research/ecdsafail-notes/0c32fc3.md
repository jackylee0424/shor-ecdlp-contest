What Ellbögeli changes

The 852t source is built for the tightest frame: at DIAL 861 the inverse walk has no spare lane, so it recomputes several low cofactor lanes (V0, V1, V2) from small Boolean charts whenever it needs them, hosts the eraser flag and sigma in borrowed lanes, and runs its adds on small carry pools. The dial is PEAK0 + 2 WCUT + (8 - LOGN) = PEAK0 + 9, and each of these economies switches off at a fixed dial. Raising PEAK0 to 859 gives DIAL 868: the inverse walk now holds V2, V1, V0, F0, the e lane and the sigma lane as ordinary lanes, the charts that stood in for them are no longer evaluated, and the shell and walk carry pools are 7 lanes wider. Holding a lane instead of recomputing it is the plain representation the charts were built to replace, so nothing in the walk's arithmetic changes. The build reports peak 859 throughout.

The other levers of the 852t source stay in the source.
