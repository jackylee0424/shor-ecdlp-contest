## Method

The research ran as an automated loop with the following roles.

1. **Parallel model workers on separate areas.** Workers (claude-opus-5-5, claude-sonnet-5-5, gpt-6-astra, gpt-6.1-sol, all at high effort) were assigned disjoint areas: the divide walk, the multiply walk, a merge/integration area, and a failure-rate (λ) modelling area. Each worker proposed ideas, built candidates from source in `src/point_add`, and logged every start and finish to a shared idea log. A director (Claude Opus 5.5) assigned areas and read results.
2. **Independent verifier.** A candidate is qualified only after an independent run on fresh random shots, paired against its parent, with 1M paired random shots as the verifier's reference measurement. Gains are priced conservatively, with the paired SE and a minimum three-event floor charged against the Toffoli delta.
3. **Integrator.** A merge worker decides which qualified leaves are combined, using only predeclared, frozen compositions, and re-verifies the combined stream. It applies no resampling and no new policy at merge time.
4. **GPU nonce grinder.**
   - It compiles a generic bit-sliced screen directly from the op stream of the final candidate, so the screen is not hand-written per circuit.
   - It runs at about 3.5k nonces/s per RTX A6000.
   - Nonces that pass the screen on all shots are re-checked by the exact CPU evaluator.
   - Only an exact-CPU pass counts. The screen is a filter and never the final judge.

## Changes

### Ingredient: consistent 4-bit first-round window with matched rederived and inverse phases (KH4, E224)

**What changed.** The tick0 equal-sign veto compared a 12-bit source-magnitude window against a threshold. We use a consistent 4-bit window on the independently qualified parent E193. All six bound-comparison bodies are edited:

- the initial full computation,
- the rederived full computation,
- the held-q phase repair,

each in both the divide walk and the multiply walk. The choice kernel, record format, later-round schedule, bound transformations, census policies, and every ordered HMR/R/classical outcome write are retained.

**Why it saves Toffolis.** The comparison now runs only on the retained high bits against the same strict threshold. The constant setup for the threshold (p/5 versus p/3) is matched exactly. Removing the low carry work implements the same strict threshold on the high bits, so the low carry chain is no longer computed. No low HMR is replaced or shifted. Zero scratch measurements remain at their original positions. Existing nested same-outcome scopes in the last phase body remain, including empty scopes left after deleting their basis operation.

**Why it is correct (and what is only statistical).**

- Each original and revised body was independently checked on all 8,192 source-window/selector assignments, with every scratch zero and dirty output. Quantum data and constants are restored. All scratch cleans. Full comparisons have zero phase. Phase-only bodies implement the revised carry predicate.
- The only dependence on scratch measurement outcomes is linear in phase. The zero, all-one, and every unit-basis assignment therefore proves all 4,096 (full) or 2,048 (phase) measurement assignments.
- A positive control (KH12) reproduces the parent operation stream exactly.
- The final pass checks the full E193 pre-tail length and SHA3 and a complete 4-full / 2-phase structural census.
- A clean, deterministic, empty-environment `qbuild` reproduces every semantic operation of the independent construction.
- This proves the changed local function, **not whole-circuit equivalence**. The window truncation changes which shots trip the veto, so it is a statistical approximation and the failure rate is measured, not proven.

An actual-rule, ideal-width classical model, using independent 1M denominators, was used for discovery only. It does not model quantum phases or width-envelope failures.

**Measured (against parent E193, four predeclared stages).** Direct comparisons against E193 ran at 16k (seed 6301), 262k (seed 6307), and 1M (seed 6311). A final independent 4,194,304-shot run (seed 6313, four threads) gave:

## Failure rate and nonce

On random inputs the circuit has an estimated λ ≈ 20.32 failing shots per 9,024 (verified λ above). A random nonce therefore yields all-pass with Poisson probability about e^(−20.32) ≈ 1.5×10⁻⁹, i.e. roughly 6.7×10⁸ nonces expected per success. At about 3.5k nonces/s per RTX A6000 that is on the order of 1.9×10⁵ GPU-seconds. This is arithmetic from the stated λ and rate, not a record of the actual search effort.

The nonce was found by grinding:

1. The GPU screen is a generic bit-sliced evaluator compiled from the final op stream, so it works on whatever stream is current.
2. Candidates passing the screen on all 9,024 shots go to the exact CPU evaluator.
3. The reported nonce `68224250142751` passed the exact CPU check, giving 0 classical, 0 phase-batch, and 0 ancilla-batch failures.

On fresh inputs this circuit still fails at the λ rate above. The nonce only fixes the outcome for the evaluated shot set.

Context from the idea log on where λ comes from (λ-modelling worker, at an exchange rate of 500 T per λ): the multiply walk contributes about 7.2 λ (4.7 non-convergence, 2.5 width overflow), the payload about 1.8, and the divide walk about 8.3. These components do not sum to the full 20.32 and are listed only as the model's decomposition.

## Failed attempts

The most informative failures from the idea log, as recorded:

- **Exact low-state T0 precompute (E254/E255, divide).** The old 15 T precompute is actually 8 CCX plus 7 retained products in E193. The exact rank audit was inconclusive, and the final form still needs 8 CCX plus persistent products. No candidate was built.
- **Period-8 / periodic-half matching searches (E253–E287, divide).** Many SAT/CSP searches looked for a cheaper structured low-state interface.
  - E272 completed a full UNSAT proof (566.7 s, 7,560 order profiles) but only for the native-256 period-8, rank ≤ 64 class. Audit E276 independently confirmed it.
  - Most broader queries (E257, E258, E261, E264, E282, E287) ended as "unknown"/timeouts around 900 s. E253 was SAT at n = 8 and unknown at n = 16.
  - A correction (E253) found one symmetry holds only for n = 8, so no circuit used an invalid assumption.
  - None produced a candidate. Timeouts were never treated as impossibility.
- **Exact CCZ/CCX phase factoring (E273, E281, E283, E287, multiply).** Exact shared-pair, dirty-target, guarded-Clifford, and transported-CCX sharing factorings. Small-control proofs passed, but the whole-circuit result was byte-identical to the parent (E268), i.e. zero saving. The unbounded transported-phase search (E286) was resource-inconclusive, and a bounded variant (E294) followed.
- **Post-choice cleanup classes (E271).** Five exact cleanup classes were byte-identical to the baseline.
- **Uniform-prior bounds on choice functions (E274, E285).** The minimum Hamming distance over all 9,920 five-input functions was 4/32, with the four winners already present in E272. For all 683,008 rank ≤ 2 functions the minimum was 16/128, with 2 optima and none outside the fixed top choice. These are theory only, with no circuit claim.
- **Choice-rule model (λ worker, E11).** Using v=2 "deep" iff [a > 4b] instead of the shared [a > 2b] cuts modelled walk failures from 806 to 554 per 1M walks (≈ −4.7 λ). It needs a z2-muxed b window (~ℓ T per chain, ~7,000 T total), so it is dead at 500 T/λ. Top-bit approximations gave 0. Segment/d3/d1 `choice_narrow` refinements of a parent also lost at 4M.
- **Terminal copy MBU (E201, E211).** dT 0 at 1,048,576 shots, with no qualification.
- **Affine magnitude-free T0 (E284).** The model gate was False (point estimate 115.968, conservative 182.211, minimum novel 402), so no circuit was built.
- **Quartic/DAG phase SAT (E288, E290, E291).** The two residual quartic phases proved universally zero as phases (UNSAT), but no source change followed. Exact-DAG product resynthesis found 0 universal UNSAT proofs among 512 candidates. Sample-based results were discovery-only.
- **Dependent leaves that did not clear gates.** A fixed choice-affine seed round (E269) found no conservative winner. The KH4 native-18 hybrid and terminal-veto hybrid (E275, E279) screened fine but still needed actual best-clean qualification at the time they were logged.

Small positive siblings that were measured separately and are not part of this ingredient's claim: KH6 (E222, dT −25.0, dλ −0.004 (0.01)), and final-constant-veto variants (E200 dT −15.0; E198 and E199 dT −7.5), all against E193 on 4,194,304 shots.

## Limitations

- **Statistical approximation.** The 4-bit window is exact only as a local function (proved on all 8,192 window/selector assignments and all measurement assignments). Whole-circuit behaviour was measured on random shots (up to 4,194,304 per stage; 1M in the verifier) and not proven.
- **Noise-level dλ.** The ingredient's dλ (+0.034, SE 0.027) and the verifier's dλ (−0.36, SE 0.15) are each small relative to λ ≈ 20.32. The Toffoli saving is real at these sample sizes, but no precise effect on failure rate is claimed.
- **The classical discovery model** ignores quantum phases and width-envelope failures, so it only guided discovery.
- **Fixed shot set.** The all-pass result depends on the grinded tail nonce and the 9,024-shot evaluation. On fresh random inputs about 20 shots per 9,024 would still fail.
- **Independent verification is required.** The statistical risk above is explicitly flagged for the ingredient, and we ask for team verification.
- **Scope of the log.** Several log items (e.g. E284–E300) have no recorded completion in the provided material and no result is claimed for them.
- **Placeholders.** Qubits, Toffoli average, score, and nonce are filled from the final local exact evaluation. The numbers in this note derive from that run and from the verifier's report.
