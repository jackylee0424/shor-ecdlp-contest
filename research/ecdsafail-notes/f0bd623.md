## Where the three qubits come from

The 669's width is ring 257 + Q 128 + sy 8 + sx 8 + ph 1 + size pool 8 + payload 259 = 669. The 666 keeps every register
except the size pool, which drops to five lanes: 257 + 128 + 8 + 8 + 1 + 5 + 259 = 666.

- **Stages 1–8 of each column** (the column probes, the crossing middle, the digits and stage 5) hold Z's 8-bit size as
  sz = pool[0..5] ++ [sx6, sx4, sx1]. Bits 5, 6 and 7 live on column scratch lanes that the 669 used for other jobs.
- **Stage 8** ends with the inverse size probe, which clears the three alias lanes. The map then writes the new size into
  all eight sx lanes, as before.
- **Stages 9–15** (map, tail, endpoints) see only five physical pool lanes. The withheld lanes' jobs there move to other
  owners.

| Rung | Pool lane withheld | Size bit | Host in stages 1–8 | The host's old jobs, and their new owners |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 668 | 7 | Z7 | sx1 (the old sy lane, \|0⟩ at every column start) | session temps → c (sx7); erase/take selector → the zero lane; masked compare/add prefixes → X(act); HT switch w → sx7; fixed reciprocal R → a returned Q lane |
| 667 | 6 | Z6 | sx4 (column bq and middle c0, both transients) | bq → sx2; middle c0 → X(act) inside the session bodies; fixed R's c0 → a second returned Q lane |
| 666 | 5 | Z5 | sx6 (ak in gated loops, column f/act, the P1 flag) | ak → sx3 (the old zero lane); f/act → sx0; P1 flag → sx3; fixed R's ak → a third returned Q lane |

The cost is scratch width.
- A measured liveness probe on the 669 (64 shots with and 64 without payload; 68,706 intervals) found at most 16
  simultaneously live scratch lanes.
- At the 666, 13 remain. 7,566 measured intervals at the 669 have more than 13 live lanes. Every one of them had to fit.
- With any lane cut, the same intervals remain over 13, and in our envelope model no column has a ring lane that stays
  zero. So we found no cheaper cut than a top size bit.
- The 669's construction census also ranked the top size bits as the lanes with the fewest roles.

Most of the fitting uses conditionally clean hosts: lanes that are |0⟩ on every shot where the kernel acts.
- X(act) inside an act-gated body;
- ph on HR shots, where ph = 0;
- X(ph) on HT shots;
- Z7 inside an open session frame;
- Q lanes that the reciprocal returns clean.

Where no host exists, the kernel uses its exact dirty form, which needs no clean prefix and costs Toffoli instead.

## 2. The 667 cut: pool lane 6, size bit 6 on sx4

sx4 held the column's bq and the middle's carry c0. The liveness probe found no state on sx4 at any stage 1–8 mark

- **Maps and tail:**

**The lesson of the 667: one defect class, found by a census.**

The first integration repaired failures one replay at a time:
- **Column 1:** the map's frame-carry list still lent a lane that held the trailing-zero exponent.
- **Column 10:** the fixed top selector still carried on sx4 while sx4 held size bit 6.
- **Column 20:** the fixed park loop's gate chain and compare ripple carry were still on sx4.

inherited lane lists that still named the lane now holding a size bit.

- Construction then passed 408/408 with zero reports.

The prefix fixtures then found two last stage-5 defects in fixed HT columns:
- the short u form at column 141;
- at column 142, a vg record whose X(ph) carry supplied a carry-in of 1 when ph = 0, offsetting u by one. It moved to

## 3. The 666 cut: pool lane 5, size bit 5 on sx6

### 3.1 The first attempt, and the redesign

The first 666 design stopped at its pre-gates. Three obligations each failed twice:
- the endpoint's column-0 map;
- the four-lane HT digit at column 37;
- the 13-lane session forms.

We redesigned under one rule: a failure is a bug to localize before it counts. Every obligation was then repaired at
donor width, on the 669 bytes with the 666's three lanes reserved, before the integration began.

1. **HT digit on four ordinary lanes (columns 37–161).**
   - The cause was one physical lane doing two jobs: the Q divisor's zero pad and the division's ripple carry. The
     first divergence is at the first post-division mark.
   - The carry now sits on fa where the guard is act itself; otherwise it sits on act inside an X(act) bracket around
     the size restore. The fallback add's initial carry is X(act), not act.
   - The bank is {sx0, sx2, sx5, sx7}, with act on sx3. Column 162 keeps its five-lane form, and 163–202 keep the 669's
     six-lane offset form with Q room.
   - Measured at donor width: +2.64M emitted Toffoli over the five-lane digit.
2. **HR crossing digit on four ordinary lanes (columns 32–112).** The 667's bytes, on the bank {sx0, sx2, sx3, sx7}
   with act on sx5.
3. **The endpoint's column-0 map.**
   - ph enters the endpoint as 1, so it cannot be the promised-zero compare helper there.
   - The compare and zero lane moves to Q115, which is clean because R < 2^26 at the end, and the map operand becomes
     Q[..115]. Q116 and Q117 hold the exponent lanes, Q118 the flag and Q120 the replay helper.
   - Measured: +7,520 Toffoli and +22,010 ops per endpoint.
4. **Sessions in 13 lanes** (the third design):
   - ak on sx3, the old zero lane;
   - X(act) on sx5 as a conditionally clean host for one kernel call;
   - ph or X(ph) as the ripple carry, chosen by the running stage, not by the column kind;
   - Z7 on sx1 as a temp only while a session frame is open (measured zero at 22,806 frame marks);
   - X(ak) as the carry in the masked adds that run with q0 in ph.

   It passed the counted gate:
   - 552/552 session marks with and without payload;
   - 64 witnesses across all 203 columns;
   - 12 inverse replays per mode;
   - zero keep-set violations in 276 audit scopes per mode.

   Its builder count is −454,410 Toffoli per leg. That is an estimate of about −1.8M over the four legs, not a circuit
   measurement.
5. **Consumers of the old zero lane.** At the 666 that lane is ak.
   - The gate chain becomes [x128], the exact dirty compare.
   - The top-equality test takes its zero source from x128 (sx2).
   - The fixed-HR carry goes on ph, which is 0 on every shot in columns 0–23, or on X(act) otherwise.
6. **Fixed P1 columns 24, 25, 139 and 140.** The two-pass P1 kernel replaces the legacy fixed R there, so no zero-lane room
   is needed. It uses R = Q[0..23) + sz5, a dirty catalytic D and its virtual odd head.
7. **Maps on five pool lanes.**
   - **Columns 0–35 and 120–202 (plain maps).** The compare and zero lane is ph: 0 on every shot below 36, and 1 on
     every shot from 120, with X(ph) inside the record.
   - **Columns 36–96.**
     - These columns mix HR and HT shots, so ph is not clean there. Using the ph compare in every uncontrolled map
       passed 18 isolated map instants but failed whole traversals.
     - The first real divergence was at column 71: a priority-probe CCX into live ph left an odd-shot phase debt.
     - The compare and zero lane is now a clean pool lane, kept out of the free list and in the probe bank only while
       clean. The switch witness w sits on another pool lane, and the returned trailing-zero temp joins the helper sets.
     - Measured at donor width: +7.56M emitted Toffoli and +38.5M ops.
   - **Columns 97–119, the done-column maps.**
     - The inherited owners here (the compare lane and the done flag on size lanes 5 and 6) are now the map's own
       output lanes. So these maps run as controlled replays on all four legs.
     - The helper is a borrowed lane in an arbitrary state, restored exactly: four Toffoli per three-control toggle.
     - The done flag and the compare lane are distinct lanes.
     - Measured at donor width: at most +1.19M Toffoli per column-leg over the 669's map, and +83.9M emitted Toffoli
       and +40.0M ops in the whole stream. This is the largest single cost we measured at donor width. PAIR-CTRL (§4)
       takes 80.3M emitted Toffoli of the final build's controlled-replay cost back.
   - **Free list.** The map's free list is the five physical pool lanes only. A construction guard rejects any map
     helper, probe, park or extension lane that is an alias lane.
8. **Tail and endpoints.**
   - The post-map carve runs on five pool lanes (sw = pool0; tail register = pool1..4 + nsx4..7).
   - The final payload size bits 5–7 move to Q109–Q111.

## 4. PAIR-CTRL: a new exact gate cut in the done-column maps

The 97–119 done-column maps were the largest single cost we measured at donor width (§3.1, item 7). PAIR-CTRL is a new
gate cut on this 666 that takes the done control off their frame multiplies. It needs no lane and changes no owner.

**Mechanism.** Each controlled map record replays three parts under the done control: a frame multiply F, the middle M,
and then F's exact reversed inverse F⁻¹. With the dirty helper, each controlled three-qubit gate costs four Toffoli,
and each controlled CX or swap costs one.
- When F does not act on the control lane, ctrl(F·M·F⁻¹) = F·ctrl(M)·F⁻¹. With the control off, the right side is
  F·F⁻¹, the identity; with it on, both sides are F·M·F⁻¹.
- So PAIR-CTRL plays F and F⁻¹ with no control and keeps the done control on M only. A three-qubit gate in F or F⁻¹
  then costs one Toffoli in place of four, and a CX or swap costs none in place of one.

**Exactness.** The identity holds only if F⁻¹ undoes F on every input and F leaves the control alone. The builder
enforces both:
- **F⁻¹ undoes F on every input.** The paired spans now also run on done shots, where some of their lanes are dirty. An
  AND gate assumes a clean target, so the AND gates inside the paired spans are played as plain CCX compute and
  uncompute, which are exact on any input.
- **F leaves the control alone.** The done flag and the dirty helper never appear in the paired spans.
- **Pairing by content.** The builder accepts a pair only when the second span is the first span's gates reversed and
  inverted, gate for gate. Any mismatch falls back to the old controlled replay. Only the frame multiply and its inverse
  are paired; the record's other spans stay controlled.

**Measured on the 666:**
- All 92 controlled map records (columns 97–119 on all four legs) paired by gate content, with no fallback. The paired
  spans carry 74.8% of those records' control overhead, counted as three extra Toffoli per three-qubit gate and one per
  CX or swap.
- Emission at the same width (666): 745,688,686 emitted Toffoli and 1,609,791,114 ops with PAIR-CTRL off; 665,375,958
  and 1,556,634,506 with it on. The saving is 80,312,728 emitted Toffoli (10.8%) and 53,156,608 ops. The builder's count
  of the paired gates predicted 80,312,856 Toffoli, 128 more than measured.
- With PAIR-CTRL off, the merged source re-emits the pre-merge 666 stream byte for byte.
- The strict 64-shot prefix fixtures, with and without payload, are bit-identical with PAIR-CTRL on and off at all 203
  column boundaries, including phase. Their shots mix done and not-done shots of both counter parities.
- The final-byte gates below all ran with PAIR-CTRL baked. Because the rewrite is exact, the failure model is the same
  with it on or off.
