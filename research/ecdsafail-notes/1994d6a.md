# Greedyfrog: exact reflection routing and directly matched parked AND reuse

This circuit retains the published SnehalRaj parent's greedyfrog traversal and arithmetic envelope. The public parent already uses four-reflection routing. This change schedules its prefix computation and measurement-phase repair in three additional clean lanes. Directly matched parked AND reuse is retained or applied with the same strict equality rule described below. The compiled circuit has 818 qubits and averages 5239975.957 executed Toffoli gates on the unmodified benchmark's 9,024 shots. The benchmark reports zero classical mismatches, zero phase-garbage batches and zero ancilla-garbage batches. The serialized operation stream has SHA-256 508c4a18fc939304fe46e8e058e44d45a7e0113eb0f84de5b810f430f5f5c5ea.

The traversal still has 260 slots, its original per-slot windows, its original division-row capacities, and the original length registers, counter and ring geometry. No bound, arithmetic headroom, comparison width or iteration count was shortened. The two changes concern how an existing permutation or Boolean value is implemented. They do not select inputs on which the arithmetic runs. The exactness claim is relative to the inherited circuit, which already has a nonzero failure rate away from its committed Fiat-Shamir corpus.

## The routing change

A greedyfrog slot renormalizes its value ring by a shift between zero and seven. The independent reference used to verify the routing obtains that shift through binary search: test four leading lanes, conditionally rotate by four, then test two leading lanes and rotate by two, then test one lane and rotate by one. Those three controlled rotations operate on a ring of 258 lanes. The permutation decomposition uses 256, 256 and 257 Fredkin gates respectively. Each Fredkin is one Toffoli and two CNOT gates. Consequently the routing alone costs 769 Toffoli gates for each renormalization, independently of the measured shift on a particular input.

The inherited reflection construction obtains all three shift bits on the unchanged ring. Let z_j mean that the first j lanes at the top are zero, and include the slot's activity bit in every z_j when that bit is present. These seven nested prefix predicates are computed with three clean temporary lanes. The fourth prefix is placed directly in the high shift output. After the first three prefixes have supplied their XOR contributions, their lanes are erased and reused for prefixes five through seven. The high shift output supplies the common fourth-prefix control for that second group. The shift bits are

    r_0 = z_1 XOR z_2 XOR z_3 XOR z_4 XOR z_5 XOR z_6 XOR z_7,
    r_1 = z_2 XOR z_4 XOR z_6,
    r_2 = z_4.

If the leading-zero count is l, the number of true prefixes is min(l, 7). The first expression is its parity; the second expression changes at counts two, four and six; the third changes at four. Thus the expressions give exactly min(l, 7), including an all-zero prefix and the inactive case. They do not assume any probability distribution of ring contents. The prefix chain uses logical ANDs, is copied into the shift register by CNOTs, and is then uncomputed by measurement with the standard phase correction. Its scratch is released before routing.

Define a reflection R_a on a ring indexed modulo n by i -> a-i. A controlled reflection consists of one Fredkin for each distinct unordered pair {i, a-i}; fixed points require no gate. On the reversed physical lane order, the following four stages implement the upward rotation by r_0 + 2r_1 + 4r_2:

    axis  1, controlled by r_0;
    axis  0, controlled by r_0 XOR r_1;
    axis -2, controlled by r_1 XOR r_2;
    axis -6, controlled by r_2.

Each adjacent XOR is formed by a CNOT into the existing control and undone immediately after its reflection. No additional routing ancilla is allocated. An isolated bit selects a pair of reflections whose axes differ by the corresponding power of two. Adjacent selected bits cancel their shared reflection, and the resulting permutation is the same rotation for all eight choices of the shift. Since the controls are restored and the equality holds for every computational-basis value, it holds coherently on superpositions as well.

For the 258-lane ring, the odd-axis reflection has 129 swapped pairs and each even-axis reflection has 128. The four stages therefore cost 513 Toffoli gates. The inverse uses the same controlled reflections in reverse order. In comparison with the independent binary-search reference, the inherited reflection construction's gross routing reduction across two forward and two inverse traversals is 256 times 260 times four, or 266,240 Toffoli gates. Prefix generation and phase cleanup have their own smaller costs, so this arithmetic estimate is not substituted for the executed benchmark metric above.

## Inverse cleanup and lifetimes

The inverse slot reconstructs the same shift word as the parent and repairs the forward slot's measurement receipts as before. It then applies the reflection network in reverse order. At that point the pre-renormalization ring has been restored. All three shift lanes are X-measured before any is reused. Three external scratch lanes hold the first three prefixes. The erased shift lanes then hold prefixes four, five and six. Each receipt controls Z gates on precisely the stored prefixes in its XOR formula. Prefix seven is needed only as a phase, so its contribution is a receipt-controlled CZ between prefix six and the complemented seventh input bit. Finally all six stored prefixes are erased in reverse dependency order. This schedule preserves the phase repair while using only three additional clean lanes.

This is the identity for erasing a computed Boolean value f: measuring its lane gives the phase (-1)^(m f), and applying the same phase from the still-available inputs cancels it. Sharing a prefix between several phase expressions only shares a computed value; all of the receipt-controlled phase terms remain. The change does not discard a receipt or assume that a measurement result is zero. The prefix temporaries are short lived and fit below the existing circuit cap. The authoritative width is the largest serialized wire index plus one, rather than just a requested allocator cap.

The emitter reserves a small amount of capacity for exact rewrites that increase the number of Clifford operations. Before applying parked-product edits, it also reserves their exact required growth. These allocation adjustments avoid a transient doubling of a large operation buffer. They change storage capacity only; rewrite decisions, operation order and serialized gate records are unchanged. The parent at its published settings was rebuilt and required to match its published operation hash before the reference comparisons.

## Parked Boolean products

The second change derives from SnehalRaj's public parked-AND lease implementation, with a stricter acceptance rule for this circuit. Only direct equality of the value-numbered Boolean forms is used. Matches inferred solely from subsequent phase corrections are disabled. In-place leases are also disabled. The implemented form parks the value in an existing idle zero wire, preserving the original measurement event.

At an eligible erase, a swap moves the product to a zero wire that is untouched until a later recomputation. The original wire is now zero and is measured with the original HMR operation; its classical receipt is explicitly set to zero. All readers of that receipt must be diagonal phase corrections, so the zero branch has no remaining correction. At the later CCX, the parked product is swapped into the target that was proven zero, replacing the Toffoli. A final X is used if the two matched forms differ by a constant complement.

The value matcher tracks Boolean forms and their constants through the operation stream. A candidate also requires compatible classical conditions, no non-diagonal reader of the receipt, and an idle parking lane over the entire interval. Reservations prevent two leases from sharing the same wire at overlapping times. No new physical wire is introduced. The direct-equality restriction means the retained value itself, rather than only an inferred successful-input relation, must equal the value that the later gate would compute. The implementation remains deterministic: decisions depend on forms, positions and wire indices, not iteration order of a hash table.
