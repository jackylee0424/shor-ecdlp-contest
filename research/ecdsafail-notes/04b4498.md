# Q867 Lilypad-GCD: guarded conditional reset/measurement rewrites

**Validation status:** the source-pinned artifact was rebuilt deterministically, matched the screened raw packet, and passed the unchanged evaluator and the official macOS sandboxed benchmark on all 9,024 shots. The hosted submission outcome is not asserted in advance.

## Scope and objective

This is a **same-qubit Pareto/down move**, not a claim of a new lowest-qubit record or a promoted product record. The baseline is public submission `37cded22-376b-4d26-83a9-7aa16b0d5105`, source `893d83bab084880ee329235952e9f7694d528b14`, at 867 qubits and rounded executed Toffoli 2,243,275, score 1,944,919,425. A candidate must have a strictly lower rounded score at Q867 and pass all correctness checks; an equal or worse score is not an improvement.

Model: **Kimi K3.1 Pro**. Harness: **Kimi Code**. The implementation was developed with source reviews, typed Jev scheduling judgments and bounded experiments. Those aids are not correctness or submission authority.

## What changed

Candidate source is `98e084ce261c58a6629e12b10a220322180aa6c0`. Only these three files under `src/point_add` differ from the public baseline:

- `cond_pass.rs`: reset endpoints acquire unique ownership in the existing `taken` set, so two `f` contributions cannot silently replace one another at the same reset and leave a correction reading an unwritten measurement bit. Direct `u` and `r` regressions were added without changing the production guard.
- `ctx_holds.rs`: deterministic joint original/hold measurement-outcome tests were added. The older seeded helper remains limited evidence rather than a universal exhaustive proof.
- `mod.rs`: the last conditional pass changes from `mfs` to `mfsur`. R2, CTX holds, and classical-wide simplification remain in their original order. The nonce tail remains outside the optimization passes.

The reset guard was motivated by a real counterexample, not by a desire to delete measurements. A pair of phase contributions ending at the same reset could allocate different fresh bits, while `replace.insert` kept only one replacement. The guard rejects a reset endpoint already reserved by `taken` and records each accepted endpoint. This can conservatively suppress an optimization; it does not license a partial rewrite or a dangling phase correction.

The newly enabled levers are:

- `u`: a CCX that erases its complete AND becomes an X-basis measurement plus an outcome-conditioned CZ on the original controls.
- `r`: an eligible reset window is absorbed, with its CCX contribution represented by a classically conditioned phase correction and a moved measurement. The original reset/measurement schedule is preserved where required.

No precision, walk schedule, round count, approximation envelope, evaluator, manifest, Cargo file or non-editable harness file was changed.

## Source and regression evidence

The reset regression covers all 16 assignments of four registered inputs and both forced random outcomes. It compares all six quantum wires and relative phase, and requires exactly one CCX demotion and one HMR. It does not compare newly allocated private measurement bits with a nonexistent original classical output interface.

The direct `u` fixture is `CCX -> Z(scratch) -> CCX`. The uncompute becomes HMR plus an outcome-conditioned CZ. The direct `r` fixture uses a CCX under an original classical condition followed by reset; it checks the moved measurement, phase correction and retained reset. Both compare arbitrary registered inputs, required clean scratch, original live classical bits, all quantum wires and phase across every joint outcome of the small fixture.

## Limitations and safety boundaries

The conditional rewrite and CTX mechanisms have bounded local proof and differential evidence, not a universal all-input proof. The older seeded CTX helper does not enumerate every joint measurement outcome. Explicit joint-outcome coverage was added for the new fixtures. Parent and candidate nonce packets change the Fiat–Shamir corpus, so matching nonce constants alone do not prove error neutrality or a clean island.

No measurement was removed merely to reduce Toffoli. Reset ownership, original live classical outputs, relative phase and scratch restoration are part of the correctness interface. No failing packet will be submitted as a clean one. If no clean strictly improving packet is found within the declared range, the candidate remains an experiment, not a record.
