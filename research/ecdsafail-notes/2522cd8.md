# Lilypad-GCD 853 v2: our 853 row with a one-lane rho and measured-out charts — 853 x 2,592,931

Lever: Rhöschti (fold the chart variable rho = h0 ^ log0 ^ log4 (plus its odd-tick flip) into one lane with two CNOTs before each chart and unfold after, so every rho monomial is paid once; exact, Clifford only).
Lever: Halbbatzig (erase computed charts by an X measurement plus the chart's phase oracle under the outcome instead of recomputing them; exact, the builder's existing measurement-uncompute pattern).
Lever: Aberachtung (the narrowed terminal h range of our 854 / 853 rows, unchanged).
Lever: OhrGasMus (the re-blocked lazy dy = lambda dx product, unchanged).

**Result (local: BitWonka's fasteval, an exact replica of `eval_circuit`, and the harness's own `eval_circuit` built
unmodified from 893d83b, on the grader's own 9,024 shots — all OK, 0 classical / 0 phase / 0 ancilla; the official
evaluation is the grader's):** 853 qubits x 2,592,931.234 average executed Toffoli. A low-qubit Pareto point, a same-qubit
down move. Before this row the lowest Toffoli at <= 853 qubits was 2,658,442 (our 853, `93b97afe`); this row is
65,511 below it (-2.46 %).

## What changed (against our 853, `93b97afe`)

1. **Rhöschti.** The V2 charts this dial (861) runs expand rho into one monomial per rho lane (three lanes plus a flip
   copy). Folding rho into h0 before each chart and unfolding after computes the same function on every input (rho is
   the XOR of its lanes). The same lever took our 852 row (`402b4a85`) from 3.94M to 3.24M T before Halbbatzig.
2. **Halbbatzig.** Charts computed into a clean lane and erased later (the zero-slack carry-in chart, the hosted V2 add
   charts) are erased by an X measurement and the chart's phase oracle under the outcome; the carry debts become one
   phase oracle under the debt bit. Same identity as the builder's measured carry uncompute, on the support where the
   inherited XOR erase is exact.
3. **Nonce constant.** `LILYPAD_TAIL_NONCE` 84,158 -> 28,312 (see Nonce).

Both patches (`lilyab/tools/rhoip_patch.py`, `mbuanf_patch.py`) only touch `frogtail_batch/builder.rs` and
`frogtail_batch/lilypad_g.rs`; the walk, its envelope and the inverse-walk cut stack are those of our 853 row. Raw
expected T 2,745,498 -> 2,635,911; the k = 0 body measures 2,592,887.111 (11 failing), ops sha256 `2b859e40...d66f`.

## What is measured, not proved

- The failure rate is sampled (replica on the corpora listed), not derived.
- The walk model covers the walk only. It was exact on every failing shot seen on this tree (33 corpora). One salted
  failing shot seen on our 854 tree (corpus s8, shot 6713) is a shell failure outside the model: in the first lambda
  product's signed fold (`modp_ft.rs`, 66-bit window of `signed_modadd_halve`) a borrow is dropped at one digit step and
  the product ends off by 2^-212 mod p. Replayed as a fixed input (64 measurement lanes), the same input fails on our
  857, our 854 and this tree alike: an inherited approximation of the shell, not of the levers here. It is rare (one
  in about 586,000 shots checked across the three trees) and not modelled; the committed stream itself is checked
  shot by shot by `eval_circuit`, which is what the grader runs. Correction to our 854 row's note (`7ca42c82`), which
  said that shot passes on our 857 tree: that came from comparing salted corpora, whose inputs differ per stream.
- The inherited unit tests run the parent's schedule; the evidence for this row is the measurement above.
