## In one paragraph

The 849 row made the greedyfrog slots budget-aware. Every site reads a qubit cap (`GreedyFrog::cap`, the circuit's
peak) and takes clean ancillas and carry pools only while the cap leaves room, falling back to borrowed passenger
lanes (Barenco et al. Lemma 7.2) below it. The same code therefore builds a valid circuit at any cap from 815 up.
This row is that code built at 825 qubits. The traversal (one greedy binary Euclid digit per uniform slot, decisions
re-derived from the cofactors), its windows, its slot count and its failure budget are those of every greedyfrog
row. The gain at this cap comes from the measured erasures and mid mask passes everywhere, larger carry pools than at 815, and parking on the later masked-row slots.

## 1. What runs at this cap

The mechanisms are described in full in the 849 row's note. In short:

- **Measured erasures.** A flag erased by recomputing it is now X-measured, and its phase repair runs in a block
  conditioned on the outcome, so the repair's Toffoli count on about half the shots. Sites: the swap flag and its
  temporaries, the swap decode's comparisons, the `d` erase, the window-slack erase, the sweep's per-row one-hot
  (the sweep is emitted directly), the rotation decode's comparators, the renormalisation flags, the step flag (a
  CZ, no Toffoli) and both done-flag erases. These cost no qubits and apply at every cap.
- **Mid mask passes.** The value and cofactor ladders keep their running mask live between the up and down passes,
  so neither pass spends interval comparators on it. No qubit cost.
- **Carry pools.** Logical-AND carries, uncomputed by measurement, on clean lanes in the division rows, the value
  and cofactor ladders and the 28-lane compare ladders, sized to the room under the cap.
- **Junk parking.** At 825 the division-rows phase has about 24 lanes of room, so junk parking runs on the masked-row slots whose junk span is at most about 7 lanes (the later ones); the earliest masked slots keep masked rows.
- **Borrowed lanes.** Where the cap binds, the AND chains, comparators, increments and constant adds borrow the
  idle passenger lanes and restore them, as in the 815 row.

## 3. Failure budget and nonce

The decode, the windows and the slot count are unchanged, so the model rate is about 6.3 expected failing shots per
9,024, under the PR 419 cap of 18. Every change since the 825 row is exact on the shots the windows admit.

The tail nonce was scanned on this gate list (`greedyfrog_nonce_scan`: classical replay of every shot's two
divisions through a bit-exact emulation of the windowed walk and decode). Counting up from 1, 69 is the
first clean value of 96, and `TAIL_NONCE = 69` is committed. The trusted evaluator confirms it. A
passing packet does not lower the true rate; the model rate above is that rate.
